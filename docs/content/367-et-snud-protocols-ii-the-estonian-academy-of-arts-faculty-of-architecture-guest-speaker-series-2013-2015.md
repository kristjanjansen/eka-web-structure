Protocols II. The Estonian Academy of Arts Faculty of Architecture Guest Speaker Series 2013–2015”.")

![](https://www.artun.ee/app/uploads/2018/09/SNUD-II-cover-2-900x900.jpg)

„Protokollide” II osa koondab Eesti Kunstiakadeemia arhitektuuriteaduskonna avatud loengute sarjas 2013–2015 esinenud külalislektoritega tehtud intervjuud, mis on enamjaolt (sageli lühendatud kujul) avaldatud ka eesti meedias. Loengusarja raames on Tallinnas loenguid pidanud mitmed maailmakuulsad arhitektid, teoreetikud, kriitikud ja urbanistid, et tutvustada arhitektuuri, disaini, linnaehituse ning kriitilise mõtte värskeid vaatenurki.

This is the second compilation of interviews with guest speakers at the Estonian Academy of Arts Faculty of Architecture Open Lecture Series. In line with the first edition, these ‘protocols’ are intended to reveal the operational tools, urgency, agency, risks, emergent and collaborative ways of thinking, research and design practices, that particular architects and urbanists from different parts of the world have developed and implemented in their work.
