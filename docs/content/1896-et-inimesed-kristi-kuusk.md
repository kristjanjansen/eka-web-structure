# Kristi Kuusk

![](https://www.artun.ee/app/uploads/2016/08/Kristi-Kuusk.jpg)

Guided by a mission to combine craft techniques and mindset, sustainable practices and wearable electronics, Estonian smart textiles designer Kristi Kuusk is making technology more tactile. She carried out her PhD research “Crafting sustainable smart textile services” (2016) at Eindhoven University of Technology.
