_The Leida Reader_ compiles articles from the online journal Leida, established by the Faculty of Design at the Estonian Academy of Arts and published between 2022–2024. Leida brings together different approaches by designers, artists and design researchers, ranging from craft, material and technology studies, and co-designing with local communities, to various practices of mending and care. The articles explore the devastating consequences caused by design, as well as the new perspectives opened up by design that have an immediate impact on people and their interior and exterior environments.
