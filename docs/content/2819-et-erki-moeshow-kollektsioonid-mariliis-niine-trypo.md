Trypophobia is aversion to the sight of irregular patterns or clusters of small holes or bumps. It is not considered real since it is more related to sigust than fear. The Purpose of Trypo is to explore the aversion of repetitive patterns through the insvestigation of different textures. All the items and materials are knitted with knitting machine. The yarn I used are combinations of angora, polyester, metallic and fur efect yarns all in black color.
