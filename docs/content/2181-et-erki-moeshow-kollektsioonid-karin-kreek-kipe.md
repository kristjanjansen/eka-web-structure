KIPEroosa, a very special shade of pink, achieved using rhodamine – B. Seen a lot in Muhu national clothes.

KIPE is inspired by strong women from Estonian islands, their forgotten legacy and mythical stories. The most important article of clothing for woman was her headpiece, which could be found in many fascinating shapes and sizes. Sarcastic humor comes with island’s laborious life.

Every set conveys a hidden story just as our national clothes did back in the day.
