Collection “Stories. Part I.” is the manifestation of the family values through sustainable attitude. Latvian core and identity approaching the world with reminder of the personal value, to keep in mind the most important things, our roots, traditions, emotional values and personal feelings about the family.

The history are laid in layers of fabric, the ruffles and the long life of our clothes which are altered and adjusted for man or woman depending on the situation. The clothes were not thrown away but worn until they died themselves.
