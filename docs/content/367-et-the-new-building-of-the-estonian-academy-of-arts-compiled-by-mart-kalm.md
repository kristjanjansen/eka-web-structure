![](https://www.artun.ee/app/uploads/2018/09/EKA_UUS_MAJA_esikaas_ENG-600x900.jpg)

The compendium “The New Building of the Estonian Academy of Arts” was published to mark the occasion of moving into the new building on the edge of Kalamaja district. The book shed light on the turbulent story of its construction during the 21st century and the former Rauaniit factory building.

Compiled by Mart Kalm  
Editor Merilin Talumaa  
Supervisor Neeme Lopp

Translator Juta Ristsoo  
Proofreading by Michael Haagensen  
Design by Stuudio Stuudio  
Cover photos Tõnu Tunnel

206 pages, in English  
Estonian Academy of Arts, 2018  
ISBN 978-9949-594-67-2
