T4EU **High Leadership Training Programme**, takes place on May 5th as **online** **training session**, and is dedicated to the vital theme of Time Management.

The Transform4Europe High Leadership Training Programme is a strategic initiative designed to empower institutional leaders across the Alliance through hands-on engagement with the development of a joint leadership and governance model, grounded in a knowledge entrepreneurial mindset. Participants will explore advanced leadership models while strengthening their capacity to lead organizational transformation and fostering alignment across institutions.

This training seminar will be led by João Brás, an experienced coach and facilitator, who will **guide participants through practical and reflective approaches to managing time and priorities in today’s rapidly evolving leadership landscape.**

**Seminar Details**

Title: **Time Management**  
Date: **May 5th, 2025**  
Trainer: João Brás  
Duration: 9-13h PT  
Format: **\[online:**[link for participation](https://us02web.zoom.us/j/86215541878#success)\]

**Course Description & Learning Objectives**

In an era where the fluidity of change demands new time management approaches, this seminar invites leaders to rethink how they set priorities, manage distractions, and create space for focused work. Key learning objectives include:

Setting SMART goals  
Applying prioritization techniques  
Mastering time scheduling  
Managing distractions  
Addressing procrastination

 **Main Topics**

**Participants will explore:  
The evolving nature of time and priority management  
Tools and techniques for setting and defending priorities  
The influence of others’ timelines on personal effectiveness  
Strategies to maintain focus and reduce interruptions  
The psychology of procrastination and sustainable habits**

João Brás is a seasoned consultant and facilitator specializing in leadership, business development, and human resources. With over 25 years of experience, he supports individuals and organizations in transformation processes across various sectors and countries. João is also a certified coach (ICF-PCC and EMCC Senior Practitioner) and visiting professor at Católica Porto Business School, teaching Leadership, Negotiation, and Communication. He is known for his clear, human-centered, and practical approach to lifelong learning and impact.

“Life is already too complex for us to be creating more knots, more obstacles and difficulties. That is the only way, to me, that life makes sense. Oh. And I hate being late.” – João Brás
