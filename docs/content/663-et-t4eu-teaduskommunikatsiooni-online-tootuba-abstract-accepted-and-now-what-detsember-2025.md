Olete oodatud Transform4Europe (T4EU) teaduskommunikatsiooni kursusele **“Abstract accepted: And now what? Non-verbal strategies and effective language use in conference talks”**. Online töötoad toimuvad kahes osas, **16.-17. detsembril kell 9:30-11:30 a.m. (CEST)**  
  
– Abstract accepted: And now what? **Nonverbal strategies for impactful talks**, 16. detsembril kell 9:30-11:30 a.m. (CEST)  
– Abstract accepted: And now what? **Effective language use in conference talks**, 17. detsembril kell 9:30-11:30 a.m. (CEST)  
  
Kursusele on vajalik [eelregistreerimine.](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe-8F41dsx4gr9EN_sMUmhwgRW6JLjCh_A2oge1aJgo8hOHjw/viewform)
