The collection “KÜÜ” talks about old Estonian ethnic practices. People used to keep home-snakes to have a continuous luck in their household and in return the master gave the snakes every morning a bowl of milk. Whistling once or twice, and from every possible corner snakes would crawl to eat, they would even creep underneath the masters coat.
