the story of reconnecting. it is time to shine

“(v)algus” is a story of an holistic street gang’s unlimited power. The animistic belief of living nature meets the dream constructors. Their garments act as an “ice breaker” in social situation. Shiny/mutable surfaces and protective magic patterns makes the viewer question the surface, indicating them wanting to touch it. Putting on a healing garment will create a ritual action, giving strength to step onto the street glowing with love.

My purpose is to create garments which bring more communication in real life.
