## Denisa Štefanigová

Audiodiary 18.5.–10.4.2020  
Voice recording  
54 min 01 sec  
Tutor: Merike Estna

[/app/uploads/2020/05/Audiodiary-18.5.-10.4.-2020-1.mp3](/app/uploads/2020/05/Audiodiary-18.5.-10.4.-2020-1.mp3)

I had the opportunity to choose something I would have to do for 30 days. I knew I had to solve some problems in myself – about painting, about my personal life. I took the notebook I received and began writing a diary.  
I decided to read the written content in order to share the emotions with my voice, as a significant “weapon” of a person also indicating his/her uniqueness. Even if you don’t see me, my presence is present precisely because of my voice and your auditory sense.  
I read from end to beginning, because in our thoughts, memories we always go back, in random order we layer them all into each other, on each other, over and through. In general, thinking itself seems to me that it works as if it did not know the linear passage of time. I have never read my notes in this exact reverse way before, and not so soon, right after they were written.

.
