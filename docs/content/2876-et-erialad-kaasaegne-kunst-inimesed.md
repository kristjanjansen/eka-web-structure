**Õppejõud ja külalised**  
Kaasaegse kunsti magistriprogrammi juhivad [Mart Vainre](https://martvainre.com) ja [Merike Estna](https://cca.ee/kunstnike-andmebaas/merike-estna), külalisdotsendina töötab [Karel Koplimets](https://www.karelkoplimets.com). Individuaalse kunstipraktika juhendajate hulka kuuluvad [Marge Monko](http://www.margemonko.com/), [Liina Siib](https://liinasiib.com/), [Taavi Talve](https://cca.ee/en/artists-database/taavi-talve), [Jaan Toomik](https://cca.ee/en/artists-database/jaan-toomik), [Kristi Kongi](https://www.kristikongi.com/), [Tuukka Kaila](https://tuukkakaila.com), [Reimo Võsa-Tangsoo](https://cargocollective.com/reimovosatangsoo), [Paul Kuimet](https://paulkuimet.ee/), [David K. Ross](https://www.inferstructure.net/David-K-Ross), [Laura Põld](https://laurapold.com), [Eve Kask](https://evekask.com), [Holger Loodus](https://www.holgerloodus.net/), [Sirja-Liisa Eelma](https://cca.ee/kunstnike-andmebaas/sirja-liisa-eelma), [Camille Laurelli](https://cca.ee/en/artists-database/camille-laurelli), [Sten Saarits](https://stensaarits.ee/).

Kaastööd teevad või on teinud külaliskunstnikud ja -kriitikud: [Anu Vahtra](http://anuvahtra.com), [Maris Karjatse](https://www.foku.ee/member/maris-karjatse/), [Bita Razavi](https://bitarazavi.com/), [Mare Tralla](https://www.tralla.net/), [Roberta Lima](http://www.robertalima.com/), Post Brothers, [Lieven Lahaye](https://cataloging.xyz/), [DOM Gallery](https://www.instagram.com/domgalerija/?hl=en), Corina Apostol, [Margit Säde](https://cca.ee/en/artists-database/margit-sade), [Tamiko O’Brien and Mark Dunhill](http://www.dunhillandobrien.co.uk/), Nicolas Bourriaud, Taavi Piibeman, [John Grzinich](https://maaheli.ee/main/), [Merike Estna](http://www.genialmythcraft.com/), [Maria Erikson](http://www.mariaeriksonartworks.com/), [Valentinas Klimašauskas](http://selectedletters.lt/), [Katarina Weslien](https://www.katarinaweslien.com/), [Laura Pappa](https://www.laurapappa.xyz/), Rebecca Duclos, [Sky Goodden](https://momus.ca/author/skygoodden/), [Rustan Söderling](https://www.rustansoderling.com/), [Kasia Fudakowski](https://www.kasiakasia.com/), [Eva Weinmayr](https://evaweinmayr.com/), [Isabelle Sully](https://www.isabelle-sully.com/), [Genevieve Yue](https://www.genevieveyue.com/), Gustav Kalm, Charlotte Biszewski, Else Lagerspetz, [Kristina Õllek](https://kristinaollek.com/), [Sandra Kosorotova](https://sandrakosorotova.com/).
