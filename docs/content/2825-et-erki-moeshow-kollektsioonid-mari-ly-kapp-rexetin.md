The collection REXETIN is a combination of aging, medicine and grim. The goal is to use different types of make-up techniques (wounds, wrinkles, dyeing the skin) and attaching it to medical techniques and materials. The sets in the collection are a mix of people’s personal stories about the subject. Example: beet color is derived from a grandmother’s make-up tool, which was a beet, example 2: a scarecrow- grandfather’s false diagnosis, which is an large arm on her stomach.
