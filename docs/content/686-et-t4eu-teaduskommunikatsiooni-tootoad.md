![](https://www.artun.ee/app/uploads/2025/03/UP_12_Science-communication-banner-1440x600.jpg)

Kutsume teid osalema **T4EU teaduskommunikatsiooni töötubades**. Oodatud on kõik EKA **üliõpilased, teadurid kui ka õppejõud**. Töötoad toimuvad _Zoomis_ **10.04** ja **22.04** kell 09.00-11.00 **(CEST).**

-   -   [Workshop 1:](https://transform4europe.eu/events/webinar-research-data-sharing-as-part-of-open-science-communication/) _[Research Data Sharing as Part of Open Science Communication](https://transform4europe.eu/events/webinar-research-data-sharing-as-part-of-open-science-communication/)_

-   -   [Workshop 2:](https://transform4europe.eu/events/webinar-effective-science-communication-with-social-media/) _[Effective Science Communication with Social Media](https://transform4europe.eu/events/webinar-effective-science-communication-with-social-media/)_

Huvilistel palume [registreerida](https://forms.office.com/e/51EVXg4mYq), teile saadetakse osalemiseks link. Rohkem infot leiate [siit.](https://transform4europe.eu/science-communication-workshops/)
